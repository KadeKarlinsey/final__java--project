About the data

Makes and models are fetched live from the U.S. government’s NHTSA vehicle database (vPIC API) every time you search.

The NHTSA database does not list cars for sale, so the year, mileage, price, color, and transmission on each listing are generated sample data for this demo. Nothing here is a real offer.
